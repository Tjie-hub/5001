## ⚠️ Amendment — correction of an error in the original card

**The original version of this card recorded the lower limit (ARB) as −10% across all tiers.
That is wrong.** The current ARB is **−15%**, set by **SK Direksi BEI Kep-00003/BEI/04-2025,
effective 2025-04-08**.

**Source of the error:** the fetched IDX English "Trading Mechanism" page rendered "10% below the
Reference Price". Whether that page is stale, whether it refers to a superseded regime, or whether
the extraction misread it, **could not be resolved**. The independent regime reconstruction
(LC-PM-0009), the SK number, and the task brief all agree on **−15%**, so −15% is recorded here and
the IDX page is flagged as a **conflicting source**.

**This conflict is itself a finding** and is recorded rather than resolved, per ADR-L1-008 (record,
don't resolve) and §7 of [[LITERATURE_RESEARCH_STANDARD]]. **B9 · citation cascade** applies: an
official-looking primary page disagreed with the operative decree. Any future work quoting IDX web
documentation for band values must verify against the SK.

## identified_mechanisms

No mechanism is claimed by the source. It supplies the **substrate** every transported mechanism
must be evaluated against.

### Fact 1 — order-driven, **no designated market maker**

Regular Market matches on **price priority → time priority** (JATS-NEXT-G / INET). The documentation
specifies **no specialist, no designated market maker, no quoting obligation.**

**Consequence:** LC-PM-0001, LC-PM-0002 and LC-PM-0004 all locate the constraint in a *designated*
intermediary. **That participant class does not exist on IDX.** Under R9 the mechanism is not
transported at reduced magnitude — it is not transported. Only LC-PM-0003 (Taiwan) and LC-PM-0005
(de facto providers) survive.

### Fact 2 — **asymmetric** auto-rejection bands (current regime)

| Reference price | ARA (upper) | ARB (lower) |
|---|---|---|
| Rp 50 – 200 | **+35%** | **−15%** |
| > Rp 200 – 5,000 | **+25%** | **−15%** |
| > Rp 5,000 | **+20%** | **−15%** |
| IPO, first trading day | 2× the above | 2× |

Applies uniformly to Papan Utama, Papan Pengembangan, Papan Ekonomi Baru, plus ETF and DIRE.
**Full regime history: LC-PM-0009.**

Other verified facts: pre-opening session forms the opening price; lot = **100 shares**; Regular
Market settlement **T+2**; short-selling exists only via a restricted eligibility list republished
2023-04-03 and is **not a practical retail instrument**.

### Fact 3 — **Papan Pemantauan Khusus uses periodic call auction, not continuous trading**

Special Monitoring Board securities trade by **full call auction**, with limits of ±Rp1 for prices
Rp1–10 and **±10% symmetric** above Rp10, and a **minimum price of Rp1** versus Rp50 in the regular
market.

**Consequence:** these are a **different market mechanism**. Pooling them with continuous-market
stocks pools two price-formation processes. **Mandatory exclusion** in any limit study.
