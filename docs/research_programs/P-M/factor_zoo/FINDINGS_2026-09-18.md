# Clean-Slate Alpha Search — Can Anything Beat 6-7%/yr?

**Date:** 2026-09-18 · **Scripts:** `scripts/` (5 files, `SHA256SUMS.txt`)
**Mandate:** ignore prior findings, test every strategy the data supports, report data gaps.
**Panel:** 20,152 name-months · 59 months · 780 tickers · ADV60 >= Rp1bn, price >= Rp50
**Status:** exploratory. Nothing registered. No spec changed.

> Note on method: a clean-slate *search* does not reset the *trial counter*. Multiplicity
> is append-only here, so the bar below is computed over 92 trials, not 23.

## 0. Answer

**No — not reliably, and not after 2025 is removed.**

The best construction found (52-week-high proximity, top 20%, with the high-volatility
decile excluded) returns **+13.67%/yr net** over 2022-2026 with **t = 1.44**. Remove 2025
and the absolute return collapses to **+2.08%/yr**, which is *below* the 6.25% deposit rate.
Its *relative* alpha is more durable (+14.77%/yr vs IHSG ex-2025, t 2.44) but relative alpha
on a falling index does not pay a 6-7% mandate.

Nothing found clears the multiplicity-corrected bar (**t = 3.46** for 92 trials).

## 1. The hurdle is higher than it looks

| | |
|---|---|
| IHSG CAGR 2021-07 -> 2026-09 | **+1.42%/yr** |
| IHSG max drawdown | −41.5% |
| IHSG Sharpe (0% rf) | 0.17 |
| BI rate / deposit | **6.25%** |

Indonesian equities underperformed cash over this window. A 6-7% target is therefore
roughly **+5pp of alpha over the index**, not a market-beta harvest.

## 2. What was tested

23 cross-sectional factors, monthly rebalance, decile sorts, long-short and long-only-vs-
universe, built from OHLCV with no look-ahead (features at month-end *t*, target *t*→*t+1*):

momentum (12-1, 6-1, 3-1, 1m), 1-week reversal, Parkinson-60 vol, close-to-close vol,
idiosyncratic vol (120d), beta, downside beta, skew, kurtosis, max/min daily return,
Amihud illiquidity, price level, dollar volume, 52-week-high and -low proximity,
distance from MA50/MA200, volume shock, zero-volume frequency, vol-of-vol.

**9 of 23 cleared |t| > 1.96 against 1.2 expected by chance.** That looked promising until
the robustness tests.

## 3. Most of it was a lottery-ticket illusion

Raw decile means are dominated by a handful of explosive names. The median test separates
real dispersion from tail artefacts:

| signal | mean excess %/mo | **median excess** | t (median) | top 1% share of return | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **hi52** (52-wk high) | +1.98 | **+1.63** | **3.69** | 75% | survives |
| px (cheapest decile) | +2.22 | +1.23 | 2.79 | 79% | survives, but see below |
| ma50 | +1.85 | −0.43 | −0.61 | 87% | **dies** |
| mom1 | +1.68 | −0.55 | −0.78 | **96%** | **dies** |
| skew60 | +0.67 | −0.34 | −0.87 | 232% | **dies** |

**The cheapest-decile "edge" (+26%/yr headline) is not investable.** Median return exactly
**0.00%**, win rate **35.5%** (worse than the universe's 40.4%), 29% of names pinned at the
Rp 50 floor, and the top 1% of rows carry 79% of all return (mean ex-top-1%: +0.48%). It is
a portfolio of lottery tickets whose t-statistic is not trustworthy.

Two artefact hypotheses were tested and **rejected** — the effect is not explained by either:
- **Bid-ask bounce** (Blume-Stambaugh). Re-measured open-to-open so no formation-day price is
  ever a transaction price: px actually *strengthened* (+2.40 → +2.63%/mo). Trend signals lost
  only 7-23%.
- **Attrition inside the measurement.** Missing-forward rates are uniform across price deciles
  (4.10-4.34%); scoring missing rows as −100% moves the result from +29.2% to +28.2%/yr.

## 4. The one survivor, and its honest numbers

**52-week-high proximity, top 20%, high-vol decile excluded**, monthly, equal weight,
~58 names, turnover 0.39/mo, costed at 0.60% round trip:

| | net %/yr | t | Sharpe | maxDD |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ALL 2022-2026 | **+13.67** | **1.44** | 0.69 | −34.5% |
| **EX-2025** | **+2.08** | **0.38** | 0.21 | |
| 2025 only | +62.66 | 1.78 | 1.78 | |

Versus benchmarks (same monthly sampling):

| | ALL | EX-2025 |
|---|---|---|
| vs equal-wt universe | +15.30pp (t 2.48) | +14.83pp (t 2.40) |
| vs IHSG | +23.64pp (t 3.43) | +16.80pp (t 2.84) |
| **vs deposit 6.25%** | **+7.42pp** | **−4.17pp** |
| alpha vs IHSG (beta-adj) | +22.79%/yr (t 3.27) | +14.77%/yr (t 2.44) |

**The relative edge is reasonably durable; the absolute return is not.** Ex-2025 this book
beat the index by ~17pp and still lost to a bank deposit, because the index fell ~15%/yr.

Vol-exclusion does stack here: top-20% alone is +9.68%/yr net, +13.67% with the exclusion,
and it improves maxDD from −38.5% to −34.5%.

## 5. Multiplicity

92 trials this session (23 zoo factors + 10 era candidates + 8 robustness variants +
51 from the earlier volatility sweep). Bonferroni 5% two-sided threshold: **t = 3.46**.

- best portfolio net t: **1.44** — fails
- best excess t (vs IHSG, all-era): 3.43 — fails, narrowly
- best beta-adjusted alpha t: 3.27 — fails
- ex-2025 alpha t: 2.44 — fails

## 6. DATA GAPS — the binding constraint

The search is confined to price and volume because **nothing else has usable history**:

| source | span | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| `ohlcv` | 2021-07 → 2026-09, 959 tickers, 1,251 sessions | **the only deep dataset** |
| `stockbit_keystats` (PE, PBV, ROE, EPS) | **2026-04 → 2026-09 only**, median 1 obs/ticker | unusable for backtesting |
| `stockbit_keystats.market_cap` | **0% populated** | no size factor |
| `ownership_composition` | **0 rows** | no shares outstanding, no float |
| sector labels | `candidate_watchlist_snapshot.sector` all NULL; `sector_rotation.py` 82/959 | no sector factor, no sector-neutral construction |
| `broker_flow` / `stockbit_flow_bars` | 2025-01 → 2026-09 (~1.75 yr) | too short for cross-sectional work |
| `ticks` | 2026-04 → 2026-09 (101 sessions) | microstructure only |
| `corporate_actions` | full span, 501 tickers | usable (splits/dividends) |
| `idx80_membership_history` | 640 rows, 101 tickers | usable (index-effect studies) |
| `suspension_events` | 2022-04 → 2026-07, 956 tickers | usable |

**What this rules out entirely:** value (P/B, P/E, earnings yield), quality (ROE, ROA,
accruals, profitability), size (market cap), investment/asset growth, analyst revisions,
earnings surprise/drift, dividend yield, float/ownership, and any sector-neutral or
sector-rotation construction. Those are the best-documented equity factors globally and
**not one of them is testable here**.

**The single highest-value data acquisition is a historical fundamentals panel** —
quarterly PE/PBV/ROE/market cap per ticker back to 2021. Without it the search space is
permanently limited to price/volume, which this sweep has now largely exhausted.

Second priority: a ticker→sector map. It costs little and unlocks both a factor and a
risk control.

## 7. Honest conclusion

Five years of IDX data support **one** signal that survives a median test: 52-week-high
momentum, improved by excluding the volatility tail. It delivers durable *relative* alpha
(t 2.4-2.8 ex-2025) and does **not** deliver a dependable 6-7% absolute return, because the
market it trades fell over the sample. Its absolute performance is a 2025 artefact.

A 6-7%/yr absolute mandate is not achievable from this dataset on current evidence. It may
become achievable with fundamentals data, or in a market regime where the index itself is
not negative — but that is a bet on beta, not a finding about alpha.
