## 1 · Evidence order (no new sweeps) — justified by POWER_MEMO v2

For every new hypothesis: **external prior (IDX or EM, ≥10y sample, published) → tradeability
filter → ONE pre-registered test per hypothesis, with expected detectability stated in IR terms
in advance (expected t = IR·√years) → paper portfolio.** No new factor sweeps. Justification
(v2 §2): IDX-specific single-country priors carry IR ≈ 0.6–0.75 → 7–22 years needed at the
census bar; only EM-aggregate priors (IR 1.15–1.34) and already-measured program records
(XP-001-B, IR 1.6) are detectable in our 4.9-year window. Local tests can only fail to reject;
discovery-by-sweep on this panel is structurally unable to clear the bar while the census
(N=266, bar 2.8575) keeps rising with every sweep.

## 2 · Standing tradeability rule (C1-corrected; every screen and test, no exceptions)

- **Entry** requires a real print at `t` (`volume > 0`; carry-forward/frozen rows are not
  entries).
- A position untradeable at `t+1` is **carried to its next real print** (return = entry → that
  print). **Delisting exits at the last print**, with a **−100% sensitivity row** reported
  beside every result. "volume>0 at exit" as a *filter* is prohibited — it is look-ahead.
- Rows with period returns below −95% (mechanical corporate-action drops) are excluded from
  means and reported separately until the corpus carries issuance adjustments.
- A **tick-size-aware cost line** is reported beside the frozen 0.60% RT on every result table.
- The rule is frozen as of this proposal's adoption; the POWER_MEMO v2 §1 re-measurements
  (computed before adoption) are convention-hopped observations, not discoveries — tests under
  the frozen rule start fresh.

## 3 · One universe, one benchmark
