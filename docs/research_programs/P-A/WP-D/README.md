## Integrity guarantees

- **No imputation.** Every event is transcribed from a named, retrieved published source with a URL and retrieval date. Reviews or indices not found in a source are recorded as **gaps**, never inferred ([[COVERAGE_REPORT]] §Gaps).
- **Reproducible.** The CSV is machine-generated by `scratchpad/build_wpd.py` (kept out of the repo) from the source records embedded there; each row is independently auditable from its `source_url`.
- **No experiment, no inference, no registration.** WP-D stops at the event inventory. Power analysis, σ estimation, and the `DRAFT → REGISTERED` transition are downstream and out of scope here.
