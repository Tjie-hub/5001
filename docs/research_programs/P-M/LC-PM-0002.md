## identified_mechanisms

**Sub-class:** M1 inventory. **I5**. This is the cleanest available specification of the
transitory/permanent split that [[MARKET_INEFFICIENCY_TAXONOMY]] LIM2 says P-M cannot identify at
PROXY fidelity.

**Causal chain:** investors arrive **asynchronously** → a risk-averse intermediary must warehouse the
resulting imbalance → holding inventory carries price risk → the intermediary **shades quotes** to
attract offsetting flow → price is temporarily displaced from the efficient price → the displacement
**decays** as inventory normalises.

**Participant class:** NYSE specialists (designated market makers). **Constraint:** inventory price
risk under an explicit assumption that *"the intermediary cannot hedge any of the nonsystematic risk
on her position."*

**The identification method — the card's main value:** a **state-space model** decomposing price into
a martingale efficient price (permanent, information) and a **stationary AR(1) pricing error**
(transitory, pressure), with permanent changes related to *surprise order flow* and transitory
changes to *intermediary inventory*.

**horizon:** transitory component substantially decayed within **1–2 trading days**.

## transportability_condition

Three conditions, and the first is **not satisfied on IDX**:

1. **A designated, inventory-bearing intermediary must exist.** IDX has none (LC-PM-0006). The
   mechanism must instead be carried by voluntary suppliers with no obligation to quote.
2. **Inventory must be observable** to run the decomposition. The proprietary specialist-inventory
   feed is what makes the transitory/permanent split identifiable here. **No IDX equivalent exists**
   at PROXY fidelity — this is precisely LIM2.
3. The magnitude scales with illiquidity (118 bp small-cap vs 17 bp large-cap), so any IDX analogue
   would concentrate in exactly the names with the worst executability.

## weakest_link

**Without inventory data the decomposition is unavailable, and without the decomposition a
1–3 day signed-flow test measures a *sum*, not a mechanism.** At any horizon inside ~2 days the
observed forward return is (continuing permanent component) + (decaying transitory component). Their
net sign is uninformative about which mechanism operates. This card is therefore the strongest
single argument that **A/B/C cannot be discriminated at 1–3 days at PROXY fidelity.**

## biases[]

- **B8 · Market-structure obsolescence** — moderate: specialist system, 1994–2005.
- **B2 · Survivorship (dataset)** — **stated by the authors**: balanced panel of stocks present

- **B7 · Cost omission** — magnitudes are gross; 17 bp large-cap is far below IDX's 60 bp round trip.

## barrier_statement

**Compensation, not barrier.** The premium is payment for bearing inventory risk the intermediary
did not choose. Removing it would require someone to bear the risk for free. Under §6.3 this is a
fair-compensation story → **F1 exposure**, same as LC-PM-0001. It persists but is not harvestable by
a participant who is not actually supplying immediacy.

## limitations

Specialist-only focus may overstate pressure if other suppliers were active; assumes no hedging of
non-systematic risk; balanced-panel selection; NYSE-specific structure.
