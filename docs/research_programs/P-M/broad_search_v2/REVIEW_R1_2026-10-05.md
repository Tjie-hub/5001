## 2 · Stale-open finding — the valid-open restriction is REJECTED

ZCode's claim is that `open == prev_close` on 21–34% of mega-cap days and 47–56% of liquid-panel days
shows the corpus `open` field is degraded, so X1's primary outcome should be restricted to
`open ∉ {close, prev_close}`.

**Reviewer check [verified], production DB read-only, 2024-01 → 2026-08:**

| ticker | corpus open = prev close | yfinance open = prev close | corpus open = yfinance open |
|---|---|---|---|
| BBCA | 20.5% | 22.0% | 96.5% |
| TLKM | 21.9% | 22.9% | 95.8% |
| BBRI | 21.4% | 22.6% | 95.8% |
| ANTM | 25.8% | 26.2% | 95.2% |
| GOTO | 66.2% | 66.2% | 100.0% |

Same query 2021–2026 (production): BBCA/BBRI/TLKM run 14–29% per year. `open = high = low` is 0%.

**Reading.** The rate rises with tick size relative to price. GOTO sits near Rp 60 with a Rp 1 tick
(≈ 1.5% per tick) and shows 66%; BBCA's tick is ≈ 0.27% and it shows ≈ 21%. That is the signature of
**IDX price discreteness plus the pre-opening call auction clearing at the previous close**, not of a
fill artifact. The comparison is not fully independent, because our corpus is yfinance-sourced
(`data/fetcher.py`), so it does not *prove* the opens are genuine. It does remove the evidence that
they are corrupt.

**Rulings.**
1. **The row-level filter `open ∉ {close, prev_close}` is rejected.** It selects on the outcome.
   `open == close` days are exactly the zero-return days, and `open == prev_close` days are zero-gap
   days whose frequency depends on |signal|. Both bias the slope.
2. **A-OPEN audit, required before R2.** Compare the daily `open` with the first print / auction price
   in the frozen minute-bar store (`research_data` `v_e_minute_sequence` via `query_intraday.py`, v002
   window) on every covered cell. Add a model check: predicted unchanged-open rate from tick/price and
   trailing gap σ, against the observed rate, by price bucket. If the minute-bar store agrees within
   noise, the opens are genuine and the "degraded corpus" claim is withdrawn in the handoff. **If it
   disagrees, stop and escalate to the Owner.** That would affect every next-open fill in the program
   (T1, FADE, VOLEX, the paper-trade engine) and outranks all screens.
3. **Permitted alternative (ex-ante, PIT).** An eligibility rule computed at close(d): exclude names
   whose tick(close_d)/close_d > 0.5%. Predeclare it; report the all-rows estimate as a co-equal
   lens. This removes the discreteness noise without looking at day-D prints.
4. Withdraw "spec deviation #1" from the draft predeclaration and replace it with (3).
