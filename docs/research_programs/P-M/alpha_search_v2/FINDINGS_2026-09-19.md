# Literature-Guided Alpha Search — one new positive result

**Date:** 2026-09-19 · **Scripts:** `scripts/` · Panel: tradeability-conditioned
(`vol_exclusion/scripts/trade_panel.py`), 21,046 name-months, 87.53% clean.
**Status:** exploratory. Nothing registered. All tests LONG-ONLY (IDX restricts shorting).

## 0. Why this search differs

The prior sweep tested factors I could think of. This one tests factors the
literature says *survive in emerging markets specifically*, after a web survey:

- EM-pervasive set (equal- AND value-weighted, plus cross-sectional regressions):
  **cash-flow-to-price, gross profitability, composite equity issuance, momentum**
- The IDX-specific four-factor model (IDX4) uses **operating cash flow-to-price**
  and **return on net operating assets** — *not* book-to-market and ROE, which is
  what the prior sweep tested and found null.
- Frontier-EM replication rate for 140 anomalies: ~40% equal-weighted, **20%
  value-weighted**. After costs and short-sale constraints, "only a handful".
- The index-inclusion effect is documented as **decaying**.

## 2. The one that works: dividend yield

2,070 dividends, 488 tickers, full 2021–2026 span. Trailing-12m DPS / price,
using only ex-dates **strictly before** the formation date. 46.2% of clean
name-months are payers; median yield among payers 3.33%.

| | mean | t | **median** | t |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| top-20% yield | +1.01%/mo | 2.46 | **+1.32** | **4.11** |
| any payer | +0.62%/mo | 2.31 | **+0.86** | **4.67** |
| **ex-2025** | +1.21 | 2.83 | **+1.45** | **3.99** |

It passes the **median test** — the screen that killed `ma50` (87% of return in
the top 1%), `mom1` (96%) and `skew60`. Only `hi52` and this have done so.

**Not a size or liquidity proxy.** corr(yield, log mcap) = +0.233,
corr(yield, log ADV) = +0.087. Double-sorted within size terciles the median
excess is LARGE +0.99 (t 3.02), **MID +1.44 (t 4.33)**, SMALL +0.56 (t 1.99) —
it works everywhere, strongest in mid-cap, which is also where the volatility
overlay lives.

## 3. Why this is not yet a result

- **t = 1.21.** It does not clear any statistical bar, let alone a
  multiplicity-corrected one. The wide trial count now exceeds 140.
- **Ex-2025 is +2.45%/yr**, below deposit. 2025 still does the work, exactly as
  for every other candidate.
- The mean weakens in 2024–26 (t 0.86) even while the median holds (t 2.32),
  which is the signature of thinning breadth in the tail.
- `corporate_actions.date` is assumed to be the ex-date. If it is the
  announcement date, a small look-ahead exists. **Unverified — check before use.**

## 4. What is genuinely new here

Dividend yield is the **first factor to pass the median test that is not
price-derived**, and the first to combine positive absolute return with low
turnover and a shallow drawdown. Prior value measures (book-to-market, earnings
yield) were null; dividend yield is not, which is consistent with the EM
literature's finding that the *cash-based* value measures are the ones that work.

That makes the pending cash-flow-to-price test (#3) the highest-value remaining
item in this search: it is the same family, and the literature rates it the
strongest EM value measure.
