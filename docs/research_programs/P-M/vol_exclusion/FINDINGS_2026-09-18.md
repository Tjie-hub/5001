# Volatility Tail-Exclusion — Exhaustive Falsification Sweep

**Date:** 2026-09-18 · **Scripts:** `scripts/` (12 files, `SHA256SUMS.txt`)
**Panel:** 959 tickers, 1,251 sessions, 2021-07-05 → 2026-09-17, `ohlcv.is_final=1`
**Eligible universe:** ADV60 ≥ Rp1bn, price ≥ Rp50, 60-bar history → 20,234 name-months over 59 months
**Status:** exploratory. Nothing here is registered. No spec was changed.

## 0. Bottom line

The effect **survives every falsification attempted**, including a Bonferroni bar over all
39 specs evaluated. But two results materially change what it is:

1. **It is a defensive overlay, not an alpha source.** The entire effect lives in down
   months (+0.535%/mo, t 5.72, 90% hit) and is flat-to-negative in up months
   (−0.072%/mo, t −0.65, 43% hit).
2. **It does not stack with the 002 trend strategy.** Ex-2025, 002's edge is concentrated
   in the 50–80th volatility percentile (+2.84%/trade, t 4.13); its top decile is
   −0.15% (t −0.11). Excluding high-vol names removes ~neutral trades, not harmful ones.

## 3. Costs — the earlier concern does not hold

Two spread estimators were run and **they disagree by ~3×**:

| band | Corwin-Schultz (daily) | Roll (1-min prints) | 1 tick | Roll/tick |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rp 1-2bn | 0.833% | **0.211%** | 0.683% | 0.31 |
| Rp 2-4bn | 0.827% | 0.339% | 0.845% | 0.40 |
| Rp 4-10bn | 0.781% | 0.370% | 0.820% | 0.45 |
| Rp 10-50bn | 0.707% | 0.422% | 0.616% | 0.68 |
| Rp >50bn | 0.426% | 0.388% | 0.458% | 0.85 |

Roll is computed from 63.6M actual trade prints (2026-04-18 → 2026-09-18, 101 sessions,
868 tickers) and measures **0.3–0.9 of a tick**. CS is known to be biased upward when
volatility is high and trading intermittent — exactly the thin-band condition.

**An intermediate claim made during this session — that `engine/exits/costs.py` understates
the round trip by ~2× for thin names — is WITHDRAWN.** It rested on the CS estimate alone.
On the intraday evidence the 0.10%/leg slippage assumption is defensible. The implied
34% haircut to 002 quoted alongside it should be disregarded.

**Overlay turnover is 9.1%/mo one-way (110%/yr)**, so the overlay only dies at a round trip
of ~2.48%. Net at 0.6% → +2.06%/yr; at 1.2% → +1.40%/yr; at 2.0% → +0.52%/yr.

**Capacity** (Amihud linear impact, 200 names): least-liquid construction breaks even
at ~Rp 136bn AUM (US$8.3m); top-200-by-ADV at ~Rp 334bn. At Rp 25bn AUM the least-liquid
book nets +1.86%/yr vs +0.72% for the liquid one.

## 4. The two results that reframe it

**Down-market asymmetry** (59 months, split on benchmark sign):

| | n | incr %/mo | t | P(>0) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| benchmark DOWN months | 29 | **+0.535** | **5.72** | 90% |
| benchmark UP months | 30 | −0.072 | −0.65 | 43% |

The unconditional positive expectation depends on the sample containing enough down months
(29 of 59 here). In a sustained bull market this overlay contributes nothing or slightly
detracts. Note this is **starker than the prior record**, which had up months at +0.295%;
the difference is spec detail (simple top-decile cut here vs the refined spec there) and
should be reconciled before either number is relied on.

**Path** — cumulative overlay gain +14.14% over 59 months, worst overlay drawdown −2.04%,
longest negative run 3 months. But the underlying book **lost money**: book CAGR −1.49%/yr
vs benchmark −4.38%/yr, maxDD −45.3% vs −47.8%. The overlay improves a losing book. It is
not a standalone strategy.

## 6. The three gaps — closed

The first version of this document listed sector concentration, auction execution and
survivorship as untested, calling sector "genuinely untestable". That was premature: all
three are testable with data already in the repository, and all three were run.

### 6.1 Sector concentration — CLEARED, in the opposite direction

No sector labels exist anywhere (`candidate_watchlist_snapshot.sector` is present but
entirely NULL; `engine/sector_rotation.py` covers 82 of 959). So the *substance* of the
concern was tested without labels: a sector bet must show up as unusual co-movement.

| | excluded decile | random same-size basket | paired diff | t |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| mean pairwise correlation | 0.0450 | 0.0904 | **−0.0454** | **−12.41** |
| PC1 variance share | 0.126 | 0.198 | **−0.0722** | **−22.35** |

The excluded names are **less** correlated with each other and load **less** on a common
factor than a random basket drawn from the same universe. This is mechanically sensible:
high total volatility at this end is high *idiosyncratic* volatility. The exclusion cannot
be a sector bet — it is closer to the opposite, a removal of idiosyncratic noise.

**Name persistence:** month-to-month overlap of the excluded set is 63.4%; 413 distinct
names are excluded at some point (universe ~785); **no name is excluded in ≥50% of months**
(top recurrer AYLS, 25 of 59). It is not a fixed list of the same stocks.

### 6.2 Survivorship — immaterial

62 names stop printing more than 60 sessions before corpus end; 868 survive. The dead names
behave exactly as feared — mean volatility percentile 0.642 vs 0.498, over-represented in the
excluded decile at 4.52% vs a 2.14% base rate, forward returns −4.92% vs −0.22%. But they are
only **2.14% of eligible name-months**, so removing them changes nothing:

| panel | incr %/mo | t | P(>0) |
|---|---|---|---|
| FULL (incl. later-dead) | +0.226 | 2.74 | 66% |
| SURVIVORS ONLY | +0.223 | 2.68 | 64% |

Survivorship is not a threat to this overlay. (It remains open for 002, which is a different
population; LIM-1 is unaffected by this result.) This tests only names that died *inside* the
corpus — names delisted before 2021-07 are not observable at all.

### 6.3 Auction execution — the open is the WORST window

Roll effective spread and volume share by time of day, 1.03M prints, 40 names in the
Rp1–10bn band, 101 sessions:

| bucket | prints | Roll % | vol share |
|---|---|---|---|
| open 09:00–09:05 | 14,707 | n/a (too thin) | 0.1% |
| early 09:05–09:30 | 83,266 | **0.468** | 2.1% |
| mid 10:00–11:30 | 297,021 | **0.273** | 23.6% |
| pm 13:30–15:00 | 268,462 | **0.263** | 33.8% |
| close 15:45–16:15 | 50,214 | n/a (too thin) | 7.3% |

Crossing the spread near the open costs **~78% more** than midday. Fill quality against the
day's VWAP: open median +0.000% but **sd 3.390%**; close median −0.030%, sd 1.434%. No
systematic bias at either, but the open carries 2.4× the dispersion.

**Implication for 002, which enters at next open:** its entry convention sits in the most
expensive and most dispersed window of the session. This is not an expectation effect — the
median is zero — but it is a variance the backtest does not model. For a monthly VOLEX
rebalance the fix is free: rebalance midday, not at the open.
