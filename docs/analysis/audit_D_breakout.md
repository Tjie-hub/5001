## 1. Core Principles of Breakout Trading

A **breakout** is a specific event in technical analysis where the price of an asset (such as a stock, commodity, or forex pair) passes through and sustains its position beyond a defined area of support or resistance. 

### Key Characteristics of Breakouts
*   **Area Patterns:** A breakout occurs when the price exits a recognizable area pattern on a chart. 
*   **Momentum and Trend:** Breakouts are used to identify the early stages of a market trend. They are typically followed by renewed volatility and significant price action.
*   **The Bounce Effect:** Prior to a breakout, prices often "bounce" between established levels of support and resistance. The eventual breach of these barriers indicates the direction of the emerging trend.

### Signal Generation
Breakouts serve as primary triggers for trading decisions:
*   **Buy Signal:** Generated when the price breaks through a resistance level (the upper barrier).
*   **Sell Signal:** Generated when the price breaks through a support level (the lower barrier).

## 2. Support and Resistance Dynamics

The validity and strength of a breakout are heavily dependent on the history of the support and resistance levels involved.

### Barrier Strength
*   **Frequency of Contact:** Support and resistance levels are considered "stronger" if the price has hit them multiple times without breaking through.
*   **Move Extension:** Breakouts that occur through these "stronger" barriers are statistically more likely to result in extended, long-term price moves.

### Role Reversal
A fundamental concept in breakout theory is the transformation of price levels once they are breached:
1.  **Resistance to Support:** Once the price breaks above a resistance level, that level often becomes the new support floor for future price action.
2.  **Support to Resistance:** Conversely, when a price drops below a support level, that level frequently becomes the new resistance ceiling.

## 6. Glossary of Important Terms

*   **Average True Range (ATR):** A volatility indicator used to measure the degree of price movement.
*   **Bear Trap:** A technical pattern (often found in Point and Figure charting) that falsely signals a downward trend, only for the price to reverse upward.
*   **Breakout:** The movement of an asset's price through an established level of support or resistance.
*   **Bull Trap:** A false signal indicating a stock is breaking out to the upside, which is quickly followed by a reversal to the downside.
*   **Commodity Channel Index (CCI):** A trend-based indicator used to identify cyclical trends in an asset.
*   **Fibonacci Retracement:** A tool used to identify potential levels of support and resistance based on specific mathematical ratios.
*   **Resistance:** A price level that an asset struggles to rise above; it acts as a "ceiling."
*   **Support:** A price level where an asset tends to stop falling; it acts as a "floor."
*   **Volatility:** The rate at which the price of an asset increases or decreases; often renewed following a breakout.
