## 1 · Evidence order (replaces sweep-first)

For every future candidate effect, in this fixed order:

1. **External prior first** — an IDX/EM-documented effect with ≥10 years of published evidence
   (the priors notebook is the reference shelf: Rouwenhorst 1999 EM factors; BCW 2011 MAX/IVOL;
   Hou-Xue-Zhang 2020 replication bar; McLean-Pontiff 2016 decay). No prior, no test — the
   2026-09 pattern-scan experience (12+ arms, one survivor) is the local proof this order pays.
2. **Tradeability filter** (§2) applied to the prior's construction *before* any backtest.
3. **ONE pre-registered test per hypothesis**, MDE declared from Task 2's geometry *before*
   the run (§Task-2 tables: MDE = t̄·TE/√54; broad tilts TE≈0.75 → MDE 0.29 at the D-064 bar;
   concentrated books TE≥2 → MDE ≥0.78 → untestable in 5 years and therefore not proposed).
4. **Paper portfolio** for anything that passes. **No new sweeps**: exploratory pattern scans
   are closed unless the Owner explicitly re-opens the mode, with a census priced in advance.

## 2 · Standing tradeability rule (applies to every backtest, screen, and forward input)

- `volume > 0` at BOTH entry and exit session; names failing it are dropped from that book's
  cross-section (as Task 2 computed).
- Frozen/suspended carry-forward rows excluded (the corpus's zero-volume carry-forward
  convention is banned from any new construction; REGIME-002's zero-volume guard is the
  precedent).
- Costs beside the frozen 0.60% RT: the D-059 modeled cost (tick-size-aware: 0.50% fees + ½
  AR-spread per side floored at one tick + 2σ_d·√(Q/adv20)), reported as a co-equal column, as
  XP-001 already did (its verdict flipped on exactly this column).

## 3 · Base portfolio + objective

- **Base book = cap-weighted (LW until true CW data exists — Task 3A gate) liquid universe**;
  Task 2a shows the EW book's benchmark gap is a 2–4-name-per-era artifact (GOTO 2022 −4.2pp,
  BBRI 2024 −2.8pp, BUMI 2026 −6.9pp) and unstable (2025: EW *beat* IHSG by +10.6pp), so the
  EW book stops being a primary object.
- Tilts are judged **against the base book and against IHSG**; EW readings secondary only.
- **Equity-vs-deposit allocation is an Owner decision, not a strategy target** — research
  outputs are excess returns over the base book, never absolute-return promises (the base book
  itself returned −0.40%/mo over this window).

## 5 · Design preference — broad, low-turnover tilts

- Target turnover **≤ 0.25/month** (XP-001: turnover 0.19 survived modeled cost at +0.36%/mo;
  turnover 0.86 turned +0.69% gross into −0.04% net).
- A rule whose event horizon is shorter than the rebalance period (e.g., FADE-001's 5–20-day
  events vs monthly rebalance) **needs its own cost-aware pre-registration** before any
  portfolio use — the event study's −1.5%/20d did not survive as a monthly exclusion
  (−0.596%/mo net, t −3.39), and the burden of proof sits with the low-turnover mechanism
  (event-triggered suppression), not with the rebalance cadence.
- Rationale from Task 2b/2c: power exists ONLY at TE ≈ 0.75%/mo (MDE 0.29 in 54 months);
  every concentration step destroys more power than it adds expected excess.

## 6 · Deploy tiering

| tier | criteria | evidence bar |
|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1: diversified long-only tilts with benchmark-like risk** (TE vs base ≤ ~1%/mo, turnover ≤ 0.25/mo, long-only, universe-wide breadth) | **paper portfolio on prior + consistency**: external ≥10y prior + one pre-registered forward test passing its interim, no D-064 census bar required | because the downside is tracking drift, not ruin; power math shows the census bar is unreachable for exactly the effects worth running |
| **Tier 2: concentrated / signal strategies** (single-name, event, short-horizon, or TE > 1%/mo) | **full bar**: D-064 census-adjusted t ≥ 2.8575 + forward test + modeled-cost survival | unchanged from the current standard |

Nothing deploys live under either tier without the Owner's separate deploy act (standing rule).
