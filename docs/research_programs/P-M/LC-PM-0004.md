## identified_mechanisms

**Sub-class:** M1 inventory / liquidity provision, with the **participant classes inverted** relative
to LC-PM-0001/0002 — here the *individuals* are the suppliers, not the constrained intermediary.
**I5**, with an explicit **I6** (illiquidity) conditioning.

**Causal chain:** institutions demand **immediacy** → they must offer a price concession to induce a
counterparty → risk-averse individuals, who are behaviourally **contrarian** (buy after declines),
are the natural counterparty → their accommodation is compensated → once the immediacy demand is
satisfied the concession **reverses**.

**Participant class:** individual investors as *de facto* liquidity suppliers. **Constraint:** risk
aversion plus lack of information-discovery resources — they are paid for the former, not the latter.

**Rivals explicitly excluded (five):** past-return reversal (double-sort: NIT predicts independently);
volume effects (holds across all turnover quintiles); Merton visibility (contradicted by the
*negative* contemporaneous return in accumulation weeks); serial correlation of imbalances (small);
volatility compensation (abnormal vol ~10% of average and reverts within weeks — too small).

## empirical_claims *(horizon calibration only)*

| Event | Return |
|---|---|
| Week of intense individual **buying** | **−1.11%** contemporaneous |
| Following 20 days | **+0.80%** excess |
| Week of intense individual **selling** | +1.80% contemporaneous |
| Following 20 days | −0.33% excess |
| **High-spread portfolio** | **1.40%** |
| Low-spread portfolio | 0.42% |

**market/context:** NYSE, Jan 2000 – Dec 2003; CAUD audit-trail with Account Type; 2,034 stocks.
**horizon:** **5–20 trading days.** Excess returns *stabilise beyond 20 days.*

## transportability_condition

The mechanism needs **an identified participant class**. NYSE's CAUD Account Type field is what makes
individuals separable. On IDX the analogue is `broker_flow`'s `Asing`/`Lokal`/`Pemerintah` breakdown
— which [[HYP-PM-0002_DRAFT]] §3.1 records as spanning only **2026-04-01 → 2026-08-19 (~4.5 months)**
and therefore declines to use. **Without participant-class labelling this mechanism is not testable
as specified**; an unlabelled aggregate imbalance conflates the demander and the supplier and the
predicted sign is undefined.

Note the direction of the illiquidity conditioning (1.40% vs 0.42%) **agrees with LC-PM-0002's
118 bp vs 17 bp** — two independent designs, same gradient. That is the most robust cross-source
regularity in this S1 sweep.

## weakest_link

**The sign depends entirely on which side of the trade the measured flow is on.** Aggregate signed
flow that is mostly *demander* predicts continuation; flow that is mostly *supplier* predicts
reversal. IDX's `stockbit_flow_bars` is a PROXY aggregate with no participant label
([[HYP-PM-0001]] A-PM5, carried to [[HYP-PM-0002]] A-PM2.5). **The mechanism does not specify a
sign for an unlabelled aggregate** — so an unlabelled test is not a test of this mechanism.

## biases[]

- **B7 · Cost omission** — authors are explicit that the effect is hard to harvest: *"high frequency
  strategies are quite costly to implement"*, and Account Type was not available in real time even to
  floor specialists.
- **B6 · Period selection** — four years only; authors state they cannot address long-horizon effects.
- **B8 · Market-structure obsolescence** — 2000–2003 NYSE.
- **B2 · Survivorship** — off-exchange internalisation not captured.

## barrier_statement

**Compensation for immediacy provision, with a real friction wrapper.** Persistence is defended by
implementation cost: the signal was not observable in real time, and mechanical strategies cannot
replicate individuals' implicit behaviour. That is a D3 limits-to-arbitrage story of the *access*
kind — durable while the data stays private, and **it dies the moment the feed is public**. On IDX
the flow proxy *is* public via the vendor, which weakens the barrier rather than strengthening it.

## limitations

Four-year window; NYSE-only, with authors noting *"Taiwanese results have a different flavor"*
(89.5% individual volume); causality inferred from patterns, not observed institutional trades;
first principal component explains only 1.70% of NIT variance.
