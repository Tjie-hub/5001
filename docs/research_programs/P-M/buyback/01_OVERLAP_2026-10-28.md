## Verdict

**Distinct ONLY as an executed-flow / liquidity-provision panel arm**, with two mandatory separators
(FM-over-net-payout; stress-state control). It collapses into C-003's family as a net-payout component, and
it re-skins the dead stress-reversal if the effect lives only in OJK-window drawdowns. Not a re-skin of any
closed arm at the proposed construction → proceed to B3 (power decides the tag).
