# D-065 PROPOSAL — new research order: priors first, tradeability always, {FQ} conditional · 2026-09-30

**Status:** PROPOSAL — for Owner signature. Nothing here edits `DECISION_LOG.md`, registries,
protocols, or ledgers. Justifying numbers: `priors/POWER_MEMO_2026-09-30.md` (§ referenced below).

## 1 · Evidence order (no new sweeps)

For every new hypothesis: **external prior (IDX or EM, ≥10y sample, published) → tradeability
filter → ONE pre-registered test per hypothesis with MDE taken from the POWER_MEMO → paper
portfolio.** No new factor sweeps. Justification: the local 60-month panel cannot confirm any
published factor premium at any defensible bar (POWER_MEMO §2–3: MDE at 60 months is 10–25%/yr
across bars t 1.65–3.46, vs literature priors of 3–9%/yr; the IDX value prior of 0.37%/mo needs
441 months even at t 1.65). Local tests can only fail to reject; discovery-by-sweep on this panel
is structurally unable to clear the census bar, and the census (N=266, bar 2.8575) keeps rising
with every sweep.

## 2 · Standing tradeability rule (every screen and test, no exceptions)

- `volume > 0` at entry AND at exit — frozen/suspended carry-forward rows are excluded from both
  book construction and benchmark.
- Tick-size-aware cost line reported **beside** the frozen 0.60% RT on every result table.
- Applied in the POWER_MEMO panel this changed the headline materially: the unfiltered FINDINGS
  book (+15.30pp/yr vs EW, t 2.48) becomes −2.16pp/yr (t −0.30) for the top-20% book and
  −9.97pp/yr (t −1.33) for the tercile book once frozen rows and exit-side zero-volume months are
  removed. The rule is not cosmetic.

## 3 · Objective restated

Research performance is judged **vs IHSG and vs the equal-weight universe** (relative alpha and
tracking error). Equity-vs-deposit allocation is a separate Owner decision and **not** a strategy
target; no card may use the 6.25% deposit rate as a hurdle. (FINDINGS §1/§7 showed the absolute
6–7% mandate failed in-sample because the index itself fell; that is a beta/regime statement, not
alpha evidence.)

## 4 · Open family {FQ} — conditional on Task 3 finding a PIT source

Open `P-M · PIT-Fundamentals {FQ}` (value, profitability, quality, size) **only after** a PIT
data source exists (per `data_acquisition/06_PIT_FUNDAMENTALS_FEASIBILITY_2026-09-30.md`: either
the Stockbit history probe lands timestamps, or the Owner authorizes the conservative statutory-
date build). **No registration before the data exists.** Any {FQ} registration inherits the
census and uses the MDE discipline of §1; the POWER_MEMO already shows the priors (0.24–0.37%/mo
IDX, planner-supplied unverified) are **undetectable locally at 60 months** — so {FQ} tests are
run for prior-consistency and paper-portfolio evidence, with pre-registered expected
"fail-to-reject" outcomes, not for local confirmation.

## 5 · Deploy tiering

- **Tier P (paper portfolio):** diversified long-only tilts with benchmark-like risk profiles may
  go to the paper portfolio on **external prior + consistency** (local sign not significantly
  contrary — i.e. the local test does not reject the prior's sign at the census bar). Rationale:
  with local confirmation statistically unavailable (§1/POWER_MEMO), demanding local t>bar for a
  diversified tilt would block every implementable strategy forever while adding no protection —
  the tier's risk is bounded by diversification and paper-only execution.
- **Tier F (full bar):** concentrated or signal-driven strategies (top-20% books, event tests,
  anything with TE-dependence on a handful of names) keep the full census-adjusted bar
  (t ≥ 2.8575 today) **and** the tradeability rule of §2. Justification: the concentrated hi52
  book's realized tradeable excess is −2.16pp/yr (t −0.30) — concentration converts literature
  priors into lottery tickets exactly as FINDINGS §3 documented.
