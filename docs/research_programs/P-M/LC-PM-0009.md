## Why this is the feasibility gate

The `ohlcv` corpus begins **2021-07-05**. Intersecting it with the timeline:

```
2021-07-05 ─────────────────► 2023-06-04   R1  ARB 7%    ~23 months
2023-06-05 ─────► 2023-09-01               R2  ARB 15%   ~3 months   ← too short to power alone
2023-09-04 ─────────────────► 2025-04-07   R3  SYMMETRIC ~19 months  ← the control regime
2025-04-08 ─────────────────► present      R4  ARB 15%   ~16 months
```

**Four regimes, three usable.** Any pooled study across this window measures **three different
substrates as one** and is void under B8.

## The finding that converts the threat into the design

**R3 is a natural control.** For ~19 months IDX ran **symmetric** bands, and both switches
(2023-09-04 into symmetry, 2025-04-08 out of it) are **exogenous, published, and dated**.

This is what discriminates the two rival mechanisms identified in LC-PM-0008:

| Hypothesis | Prediction across R3 |
|---|---|
| **H_band** — asymmetry of response is caused by asymmetry of *band width* (M6) | Effect **switches OFF** in R3 and **back ON** in R4 |
| **H_side** — asymmetry is intrinsic to up vs down (shorting constraints, sentiment, manipulation — LC-PM-0007/0008) | Effect **persists unchanged** through R3 |

Attention and manipulation do not switch off on 2023-09-04. **The rule does.** That is the cleanest
identification available to this programme, and it exists only because the exchange changed the rule
twice inside the data window.

**It also supersedes the design I proposed earlier in this session and then found invalid** — a
regression discontinuity comparing limit-hitters against near-miss stocks fails because the running
variable (the move itself) is endogenous to the demand process. The regime switch is exogenous.

## transportability_condition

Inverted. This card states what a valid IDX limit study must satisfy:

1. **Never pool across regime boundaries** without modelling the switch.
2. **Exclude Papan Pemantauan Khusus** (call auction, different mechanism).
3. **Exclude IPO first-trading-days** (2× bands).
4. **Adjust the reference price for corporate actions** — the band is computed off a reference price
   that a split or dividend redefines.
5. **Treat 2025-04-08 as a compound treatment** — bands *and* halt thresholds changed together.
6. **R2 (~3 months) is likely under-powered alone** and should be treated as a transition, not a
   regime.

## barrier_statement

**M6 · structural, with a monitorable decay process.** Per [[MARKET_INEFFICIENCY_TAXONOMY]] I1,
decay is a **step function on rule change**, watched on the rulebook rather than a return series —
and this card *is* that watch-list. Note the corollary: **the mechanism has already died and been
resurrected twice in five years.** A validated edge here carries an unusually high probability of
being extinguished by decree, and the correct decay monitor is a subscription to IDX announcements,
not a rolling Sharpe.
