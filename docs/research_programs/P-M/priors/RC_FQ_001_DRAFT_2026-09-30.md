## Rule card sketch

- **Universe:** top-200 by ADV60 at each month-end (UNIVERSE_BENCHMARK_MEMO pick), C1
  tradeability (real print at entry; carry frozen positions to next real print; −95%
  mechanical-drop screen).
- **Signal:** composite of value and profitability (ROE, high = long) from the
  `fq_keystats_snapshot` forward-PIT table, each standardized cross-sectionally, averaged.
  No look-back: only snapshots already fetched at the rebalance date are usable (forward-PIT).
  - **Value primary metric: E/P (earnings yield), low earnings-per-price = short, high = long.**
    POWER_MEMO v2 §2's detectability table gives E/P the stronger prior at both scales tested
    here — IDX 1990-97 (Rouwenhorst): E/P IR 0.75 vs B/M IR 0.61; EM-aggregate 1987-97
    (Rouwenhorst): E/P IR 1.34 vs B/M IR 1.15. E/P dominates B/M at both the local and the
    EM-aggregate scale, so it is the metric this card's pre-registered test is built on.
  - **B/M (book-to-market) is a pre-declared sensitivity only** — reported alongside the E/P
    result at registration, never substituted for it and never used to pick whichever reads
    better after the fact (that would be exactly the convention-hopping D-065 §2 exists to stop).
  - **Negative-earnings rule:** a name with negative trailing earnings is **excluded from the
    E/P cross-sectional rank that month** (E/P treated as missing, not as an extreme value).
    Rationale: E/P sorted naively on raw values conflates "cheap" with "unprofitable" for
    loss-making firms — a very negative E/P is a distress signal, not a value signal, and
    academic convention (matching Fama-French's parallel treatment of negative book equity for
    B/M) is exclusion, not a floor or a sign flip. The excluded name still participates in the
    profitability (ROE) leg and can still be held on that leg alone; it is not removed from the
    universe, only from the value component's rank that month.
- **Book:** top-⅓ composite, cap-weighted base return compared; **turnover cap ≤ 0.25/mo**
  (names beyond the cap keep their prior weight — the cap binds before optimization does).
- **Benchmark:** CW of the declared universe (IHSG reported alongside, context only).
- **Prior it tests:** LWZ IDX value 0.37%/mo and profitability 0.24%/mo (pending external
  verification, Task 1) — detectability per POWER_MEMO v2: IDX-specific priors are NOT
  detectable in-window; this card is therefore a **Tier-P paper-portfolio candidate** under
  D-065 §5 (diversified tilt, prior + consistency), NOT a local-confirmation play.
- **Paper-portfolio decision rule (draft):** go to paper portfolio if (i) the LWZ prior is
  verified in `priors/external/lwz/`, (ii) 6 months of forward keystats snapshots exist with
  ≥70% universe coverage per month, and (iii) the local test's SIGN is not significantly
  contrary to the prior at the census bar. Review at 12 months against the CW base.
