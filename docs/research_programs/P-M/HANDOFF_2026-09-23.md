still valid. On 09-21 it died around 15:35; on 09-22 between 12:52 and 13:35.
- The 16:15 EOD scraper then got HTTP 401 on all 958 tickers.

  came after the token had already failed.

  another device or on the Windows box revokes this host's token.

  an upstream problem.
